🔋 Graphics and performance

If your phone gets warm or the game stutters, select Battery Saver / Low Graphics in the game where available. Close other tabs and use the latest browser version. Device voices depend on installed text-to-speech support.

💾 Save your work safely

Studio workspace records are stored in browser storage and may disappear if you clear site data or open files under a different address. From Studio, tap Export backup, keep the JSON somewhere safe, and use Restore if necessary. Game progress is not guaranteed to sync between devices.

🛡️ Your safety controls

Try 4's $0 rule remains active as a design requirement. This prototype makes no payments, uses no paid AI API, and cannot access Cash App. Publishing and real business automation are not enabled.
