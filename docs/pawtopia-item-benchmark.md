The first Pawtopia release contains 49 original entries. The public market describes each item's use and acquisition path. The item definitions in `assets/pawtopia-items.js` are the source of truth for names, effects, recipes, and unlocks.

Items use different effect combinations, trigger conditions, recipes, visual badges or collectible records. No competitor names, art, or item descriptions are included. Catalog parity and a complete competitor item-by-item inventory are outstanding.
