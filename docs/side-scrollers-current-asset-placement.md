## Acceptance limits

Artwork is original **vector-painted scenery**; poster-approved feature-film-style 3D hero rigs, enemy meshes and individualized motion clips, true 3D scenery, movie audio/captions/exports, and 25 full game playthroughs are not completed by this asset integration. Source parsing and support-top checks cannot guarantee that the artwork renders correctly, that an enemy is grounded or that Android multitouch/FPS is acceptable. Do those browser and device checks before promoting a game to production quality.
