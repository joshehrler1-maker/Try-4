How these fight animations work

Each breed has its own fur and marking design, body, paw and tail details, six animated drawings per action, and a unique signature symbol. They are lightweight original vector artworks using the existing adoption pictures as face textures. They are not individually hand-painted feature-film frames.

This is a visual-only preview: it does not modify your adopted cats, spend Paw Coins, level a pet, or change battle progress. The same characters appear in the Battle Arena.
