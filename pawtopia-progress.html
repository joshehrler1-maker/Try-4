Back up your Pawtopia progress

Your cats, coins, quests, games and battle records currently save on this browser and device only. They are not linked to an online account. Export a backup periodically so clearing browser data doesn't erase your only copy.

1. Download a backup

Save a JSON copy of your current Pawtopia progress to your phone or computer. Keep it in a safe place.

2. Restore your progress
