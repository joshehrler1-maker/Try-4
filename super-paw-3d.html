Press Start Adventure to begin.
How to play: Drag the joystick in any direction to move; push farther to run faster. Release to stop. Use multiple fingers at once: move with the joystick while attacking, dodging or aiming. Start with Power Strike (X). At level 2, earn Sun-Paw Bolt / Moonbeam (Y); level 3 adds Dodge (B); level 4 adds Super Burst (A). New buttons appear automatically. Whisker uses a crouched, side-on taekwondo-inspired guard with staggered feet. Luna uses a compact Wing Chun-inspired crouch with tucked elbows and centreline hands. Both keep their own guard at every movement speed. Tap X for a heavy Power Strike: plant, pull back, rotate and punch through. Hold for an alternating three-strike sequence. Attacks wind up before contact and recover afterward. Ranged gauntlets aim before firing; enemies telegraph strikes so you can dodge. Hold an unlocked ranged button to repeat shots at its normal cooldown. Other abilities activate once per press. Keyboard arrows / WASD also work. Tap Power Strike or Space for melee. Tap Sun-Paw Bolt / Moonbeam or press F for a ranged shot; aim with the camera. Nearby visible enemies ahead are targeted automatically. Shots stop at dungeon edges. Boss warning rings show where to dodge. Collect eight crystals and defeat every dog villain, then reach the gold beacon at the north gate to finish each district. Dodge costs 20 energy and grants a brief escape from robot hits. Whisker hits harder; Luna moves faster. Earn XP, upgrade attack damage, chain combos, use energy bursts, collect potions, and defeat the dog bosses. Pausing stops gameplay and rendering. Device voices vary. No paid API or network connection is used.

District map design

Sunrise Square: sandstone courts and townhouses. Moonlight Market: striped stalls and cobbled passages. Crystal Caverns: rough stone and mineral formations. Tideworks Docks: cargo wharves above dark water. Skyline Gardens: planted terraces and trees. Shadow Fortress: bastions, banners and the throne courtyard.

Distinct room names, perimeter landmarks and branching routes support exploration. Scenery sits outside travel paths. Battery saver reduces decorative geometry and view distance. Layered landscape horizons, cloud skies, rounded structural elements, dense vegetation, irregular rocks and curved awnings reduce the boxy look. Auto mode reduces resolution and detail if frame times stay slow. These remain stylized 3D environments, not photorealistic scenes.
