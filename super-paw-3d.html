3D Rescue Quest

Move through the city, complete six city districts, recover 48 crystals, and repel increasingly difficult robot waves. Each district is a checkpoint. Completed chapters can now be saved and resumed in this browser when storage is available.

Drag to move
Press Start Adventure to begin.
How to play: Drag the joystick in any direction to move; push farther to run faster. Release to stop. You can attack with your other thumb. Keyboard arrows / WASD also work. Tap Power Strike or Space when a robot is nearby. Complete six districts and collect 48 crystals to rescue the city. Whisker hits harder; Luna moves faster. Earn XP, upgrade attack damage, chain combos, use energy bursts, collect potions, and defeat boss robots. Pausing stops gameplay and rendering. Device voices vary. No paid API or network connection is used.
