Before you buy

Can I see inside first?

Yes. Download the free three-page sampler without purchasing, and view interior-page previews on the Payhip product page.

Does the printed book glow in the dark?

No. Glow is an illustrated visual effect. Ordinary ink and paper do not glow in the dark. Suitable glow-in-the-dark art supplies would be needed for actual glow and are not included.

What do I receive?

Two PDF editions of Crystal Catacombs. Purchase and download through Payhip. A PDF reader is required; paper, printing and coloring supplies are separate.

Can I print for my family?

Yes, for your own household. The digital files may not be shared, resold or redistributed.
