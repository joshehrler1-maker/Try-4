First Ten Enemies — Real 3D Prototype Studio

Experimental low-detail models, not completed film-quality game art. These are actual shaded 3D triangle meshes with separately moving pieces for ten original Super Paw Heroes enemy families. Games 1–10 now offer a 3D enemy preview toggle inside the playable 2.5D Games Room; it is off by default, draws at most two eligible enemies per frame and falls back to their original 2D art whenever needed. Existing special enemies keep their own designs. The models are not yet approved against the master visual reference, are not full 3D environments and are not movies.
