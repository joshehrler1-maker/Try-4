First Five Enemies — Real 3D Prototype Studio

Experimental models, not completed game art. These are actual shaded 3D triangle meshes with articulated body pieces for the first five Super Paw Heroes enemy families. They are separate concept prototypes, not installed in the 2.5D games, not feature-film-quality, and not yet matched to the approved master visual reference. The existing games continue using their original game-specific animated 2.5D artwork.
